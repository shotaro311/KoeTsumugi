# KoeTsumugi notification sounds

The four bundled notification WAV files are original functional tones generated
for KoeTsumugi by `scripts/generate-notification-sounds.ps1`.

- Format: mono PCM, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit
- `marimba_start.wav`: C5 followed by E5 with a short decaying harmonic envelope
- `marimba_stop.wav`: E5 followed by A4 with a short decaying harmonic envelope
- `pop_start.wav`: an upward 420-760 Hz chirp
- `pop_stop.wav`: a downward 700-360 Hz chirp

The script, frequencies, envelopes, and generated WAV files are part of Shotaro
Matsumoto's 2026 modifications and are distributed under this repository's MIT
License. They do not sample or reproduce any third-party recording or melody.
